abstract: "If Hobbes had Twitter, he'd be a doomer boomer. My paper, Anxiety in the State of Nature: A Heideggerian Reading of the Leviathan, reexamines Hobbes' vision of human nature through the lens of existentialist philosophy. I argue that the Hobbesian man's surrender to despotism is a misguided attempt to escape the existential dread of mortality. Maybe trading freedom for security is just running from the void."
