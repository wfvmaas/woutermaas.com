title: "Rereading Kafka"
abstract: "Kafka's _The Trial_ reads like a bureaucratic nightmare, but my essay (_The Failure of Genuine Self-Expression in Kafka's The Trial_) argues that it's actually an existential one. Drawing on Camus' notion of the absurd, I examine how K. passively succumbs to his predicament, failing to assert his existential freedom. The real tragedy isn't just K.'s execution—it's his failure to revolt, to carve out meaning in a world that denies it. "
