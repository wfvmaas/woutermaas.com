title: Rereading Kafka
abstract: "Kafka's The Trial reads like a bureaucratic nightmare, but my essay: 'The Failure of Genuine Self-Expression in Kafka's The Trial' argues that it's actually an existential one. Drawing on Camus' notion of the absurd, I examine how K. passively succumbs to his predicament, failing to assert his existential freedom. The real tragedy isn't just K.'s execution, it's his failure to revolt, to carve out meaning in a world that denies it."
