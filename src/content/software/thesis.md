My thesis (*Secure MPC-Sortition: Consolidating Innovations in Democracy and Cryptography*) explores how Secure Multi-Party Computation (MPC) can enhance privacy in citizens’ assembly sortition while maintaining fairness. Two MPC-based designs are proposed: one concealing participant details from organizers and another allowing local governments to contribute encrypted citizen data. I received a 9 out of 10.
