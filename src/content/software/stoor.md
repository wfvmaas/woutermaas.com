## About Stoor

Stoor is a project close to my heart. It explores how we can use technology to preserve and share stories in a way that feels authentic and personal.

### Key Features

*   **Audio-first**: Stories are recorded and played back, emphasizing the voice.
*   **Community-driven**: Users can contribute their own stories to the collection.
*   **Interactive Map**: Stories are geotagged, allowing users to discover narratives based on location.

### Technical Details

Built with **Nuxt 3** and **Firebase**, Stoor leverages server-side rendering for SEO and performance, while providing a reactive real-time experience for users.
