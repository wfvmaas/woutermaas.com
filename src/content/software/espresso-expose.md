*Espresso Exposé* is an interactive artwork that highlights how Artificial Intelligence is increasingly reshaping our daily lives. Through interaction with a "coffee recommender" agent, the audience encounters the contrast between the direct, front-end results and the opaque, back-end processes of machine learning operations—raising the question: What exactly is happening behind the scenes?
